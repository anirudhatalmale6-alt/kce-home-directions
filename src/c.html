03  /  Quality & verification

We measure the clean, then hand you the record.

Most cleaning contracts are judged by how a floor looks at 7am. Ours is judged by ATP readings, a scored audit and a monthly report you can put in front of your infection prevention committee.

ATP testing on high-touch surfaces

Swab readings taken on a set schedule against a written pass threshold. Readings are logged by room, not by building.

Scored supervisor audits

The named site supervisor audits to a fixed checklist. The score, the date and the auditor are all recorded.

Monthly quality report

One document per site covering ATP pass rate, audit score, open issues and training currency. Sample shown alongside.

How issues get fixed

A failed reading opens a corrective action with an owner and a re-test date. It stays on the report until the re-test passes.
