Healthcare services

The work hospitals cannot afford to get wrong.

Healthcare is the standard we work to, not the only market we serve. These five services are where infection prevention, construction risk and patient throughput meet.

01

Construction and renovation cleaning (ICRA)

Containment-aware cleaning alongside active construction, from barrier maintenance through final clearance before the unit reopens.

 02

OR and terminal cleaning

Between-case and terminal cleaning to a written sequence, timed against the OR schedule rather than against a shift.

 03

Outbreak response

Escalated disinfection on call, with product, contact time and room sequence recorded for infection prevention.

 04

Shared mobile equipment cleaning

Pumps, workstations and portable imaging cleaned on a tracked cycle, so equipment that moves between rooms stops moving contamination with it.

 05

Outpatient site standardization

One protocol, one report and one standard across every clinic in the network, however far it sits from the main campus.

Quality & verification

We measure the clean, then hand you the record.

Most cleaning contracts are judged by how a floor looks at 7am. Ours is judged by ATP readings, a scored audit, and a monthly report you can put in front of your infection prevention committee.

ATP testing on high-touch surfaces

Swab readings on a set schedule against a written pass threshold, logged by room rather than by building.

Scored supervisor audits

The named site supervisor audits to a fixed checklist. Score, date and auditor are all recorded.

Monthly quality report

One document per site: ATP pass rate, audit score, open issues, training currency. Sample shown alongside.

How issues get fixed

A failed reading opens a corrective action with an owner and a re-test date, and stays on the report until the re-test passes.
