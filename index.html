Placeholders to be aware of:

  • The 20 highlighted states are a placeholder set until KCE sends the real list. Each state is one line in the map, so swapping them is a two-minute edit.
  • The shield mark is a sketch of what a light logo refresh could look like, not a final mark.
  • The phone number shown is the one currently on the site. The brief lists a toll-free number as an open decision.
  • The monthly quality report is marked "Sample" and uses illustrative figures.
  • No photography yet, by design. The brief asks for real KCE crews and warns against stock that looks like stock, so proof carries the page until a shoot happens.
  • Testimonials are designed but deliberately switched off until real approved quotes arrive.
